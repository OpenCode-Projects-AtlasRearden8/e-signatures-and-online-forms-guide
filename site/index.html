The E-Signatures and Online Forms Guide is a beginner manual for replacing pen-and-ink paperwork with electronic signatures and online forms — without losing sleep over whether any of it is legal, safe, or sane.

Every method uses tools that are free or already installed on your devices. Every chapter ends with proof you can show later. Start anywhere, or read straight through.
